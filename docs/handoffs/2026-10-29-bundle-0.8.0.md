## Deploy prerequisite
`bedwars-shop.yml` is only copied to `plugins/PulseGames/` on first run. The live `plugins/PulseGames/bedwars-shop.yml` is stale and **must be replaced with the repo catalogue** (`src/main/resources/bedwars-shop.yml`) when deploying 0.8.0, or deleted before the first match.

## Combined in-game acceptance

### Bedwars shop (Java + Bedrock)
1. Funky tab: 10 items, NEW in lore; Team Upgrades: 8 spaced entries; nine tabs readable on a phone.
2. TNT primes at once, bed next to it survives; ladders, obsidian, sponge, water bucket place.
3. Bridge Egg lays team wool; Pop-up Tower beside a bed or chest leaves them alone; ladder climbs.
4. Magic Milk: no trap fires for 30 s; otherwise one trap fires once and the team is told.
5. Invisibility hides body and armour; Jump V and Speed II durations.
6. Knockback Stick, Bed Bug (15 s), Dream Defender (fights, gone at 4 min, no drops).
7. Grappling Hook pulls and shows cooldown; Bounce Pad launches Java and Bedrock players, gone at 60 s.
8. Ice Road melts; Smoke Bomb blinds enemies only; Freeze Ball slows.
9. Rocket Boots name counts down 3 to 1 then vanish; Recall Totem channels, hit cancels, bed gone fizzles.
10. Shield Wall and Honey Trap revert; Decoy pops after 5 hits.
11. Forge speeds the generator; Heal Pool heals near the bed.
12. End a match mid-effect: nothing leaks into the next, no errors in the log.

### Spleef bots
1. Classic, 3 bots, empty arena: they walk about, never stand still for more than a second or two.
2. A bot alone at the far side walks toward you (not just rotates), within 32 blocks and behind a pillar.
3. Walk away in a straight line: bots dig a block or two ahead of you; Hard bots cut a gap in front as you approach.
4. Holes everywhere late round: bots route round them and are not stuck on a lone block.
5. Bots never fall because they dug their own block or last exit (except an occasional Easy slip).
6. Decay: bots leave blocks as they crack and stay on the dense part of the floor.
7. Splegg: eggs land ahead of a moving player, bots keep moving sideways while firing.
8. Misses are visible swings; wrong-block digs happen on Easy more than Hard.
